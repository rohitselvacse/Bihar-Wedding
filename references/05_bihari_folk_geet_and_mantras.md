# Reference 05: Bihari Folk Geet (Lokgeet), Spoken Vows & Pandit Vedic Mantras

This document catalogs traditional wedding folk music (Maithili, Bhojpuri, Magahi), live acoustic treatment guidelines, family speech placement strategies, and Sanskrit mantra integration for wedding film editors.

## 1. Master Catalog of Bihari Vivah Lokgeet

In Bihar, women’s singing is the sacred heartbeat of every wedding ritual. Below is the definitive catalog of traditional folk geet, featuring legendary compositions immortalized by **Padma Bhushan Sharda Sinha**, **Vindhyavasini Devi**, and regional folk suhagans.

## 2. Processing Raw Live Courtyard Vocals (Audio Engineering Workflow)

When local ladies sing live in the courtyard without studio microphones, the raw audio is plagued by ambient noise. Follow this 4-step restoration chain:

```
[Raw Live Audio] 
       │
       ▼
[1. Spectral Denoise / Dialogue Isolation (50-65%)]  --> Removes generator drone & fan hum
       │
       ▼
[2. Linear-Phase High-Pass Filter @ 80 Hz]           --> Eliminates low-end rumble & handling noise
       │
       ▼
[3. Parametric EQ (Mud Cut @ 300Hz, Air @ 4.5kHz)]    --> Restores warmth, body & vocal clarity
       │
       ▼
[4. Layer Subtle Ambient Indian Acoustic Stems]      --> Beds singing on low-volume (-22dB) sitar/flute
```

## 3. Sanskrit Vedic Mantras & Priest Audio Layering

During the Mandap ceremony, the family Pandit chants sacred Sanskrit mantras. These mantras should be treated as holy atmospheric textures:

### Key Mantras & Editorial Use:
1. **Mangalam Bhagwan Vishnu (Mangalacharan)**:
   * *“Maṅgalaṁ Bhagavān Viṣṇuḥ, Maṅgalaṁ Garuḍadhvajaḥ...”*
   * **Use**: Opening establishing shots of the sacred fire, kalash, and groom entering the mandap.
2. **Saptapadi Mantras (The Seven Vows)**:
   * *“Ekamiṣe viṣṇustvā nayatu... Dve ūrje... Trīṇi rāyaspoṣāya...”*
   * **Use**: Lower background music by -14 dB so the pandit’s rhythmic recitation resonates as the couple steps around the agni.
3. **Kanyadaan Sankalpa**:
   * *“Imāṁ kanyāṁ varāya tubhyaṁ sampradade...”*
   * **Use**: Father’s voice saying *"Sampradade"* (I give this child away), followed by a swelling emotional cello or sitar resolve.

## 4. Family Member Speeches & Emotional Voiceovers

To transform a standard highlight reel into a cinematic masterpiece, weave spoken words (*dialogue bytes*) over visual montages:
* **Pre-Interview Setup**: Record 1–2 minutes of quiet audio with the Father, Mother, Groom, and Bride before the rush of the wedding day.
* **Key Soundbites to Extract**:
  * Father reflecting on the day the bride was born.
  * Groom sharing the first time he realized she was the one.
  * Bride expressing gratitude for her parents' unconditional love.
* **Audio Layering Rule**: Duck the cinematic music bed by **-16 dB** when spoken dialogue occurs. Leave 0.8 seconds of musical breath before and after each quote.
