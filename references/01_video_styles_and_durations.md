# Reference 01: Indian Wedding Video Styles, Formats & Durations

This document defines the industry-standard taxonomy, visual grammar, pacing, camera work, and timeline architectures for Indian wedding films, with specific focus on modern cinematic and cultural wedding cinematography.

---

## 1. Video Hierarchy & Duration Taxonomy

| Video Style | Target Duration | Pacing / BPM | Target Emotional Arc | Primary Audio Structure | Deliverable Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Teaser / Reel** | 60 sec – 3 min (90s sweet spot) | Fast (110–130 BPM), hyper-sync cuts | Curiosity, adrenaline, grand anticipation | High-energy beat drop or modern trailer bass-riser + 1 punchy quote | Social media hook (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp status) |
| **Cinematic Trailer** | 5 – 10 min (6–8 min standard) | Dynamic: slow intro, building crescendo, energetic sangeet, emotional vidai | Three-Act Drama: Love Story -> Grand Celebration -> Sacred Union & Farewell | 2–3 track mashup or 1 full narrative track with embedded voiceovers/vows | Primary showcase film for couple, family circles, and portfolio |
| **Cinematic Highlights** | 15 – 30 min | Moderate, rhythmic, story-driven | Chapter-based journey through all pre-wedding and wedding rituals | Thematic music suite (1 song per ritual chapter) with ambient natural audio | Extended keepsake film featuring close family interviews and key ceremonies |
| **Master Film / Documentary** | 1 – 3 Hours (Full Feature) | Natural, chronological, complete | Archival, emotional, sacred, comprehensive | Live ritual sound (Pandit mantras, folk geet, family chatter) + layered acoustic score | Complete family archive capturing every relative, blessing, ritual, and reception interaction |
| **Ritual Specific Films** | 10 – 25 min each | Ceremony-paced | Authentic cultural documentation | Traditional folk geet, live vocal audio, and ritual prayers | Delivered to parents and elders who cherish uninterrupted sacred rites |

## 2. Style-by-Style Visual Grammar & Cinematography Breakdown

### A. Teaser Video (1 – 3 Minutes)
* **Goal**: Instant impact, viral aesthetic, cinematic hook.
* **Editing Rhythm**:
  * Cuts occur on heavy beats, bass drops, or snare snaps (0.5 to 1.5 seconds per shot).
  * Use match cuts, whip pans, zoom transitions, and speed ramping (fast-motion 300% to slow-motion 40%).
* **Shot Selection (Hero Shots Only)**:
  * 0:00 - 0:15: Establishing drone shot of the venue / city skyline (e.g., Patna Ganga Ghat or heritage palace), slow cinematic tilt up of bride in mirror holding bridal jewelry.
  * 0:15 - 0:35: Groom adjusting sherwani, cufflinks, sunglasses; groom vintage car or royal horse entry.
  * 0:35 - 1:10: Rapid montage: Haldi splashes in 120fps slow-mo, smoke bombs, Sangeet dance floor jumps, flame sparklers, Baraat energy.
  * 1:10 - 1:45: Dramatic build-up: Jaimala stage eye contact, bride walking down the aisle under *phoolon ki chadar*.
  * 1:45 - 2:30: Emotional climax: Sindoor Daan moment with optical flare, couple laugh, tear falling down mother's cheek during Vidai, sudden silence.
  * 2:30 - 3:00: Final hero portrait of couple walking toward camera, slow-motion smile, title card fade-out.

### B. Cinematic Trailer (5 – 10 Minutes)
* **Goal**: Hollywood-style narrative storytelling with a distinct 3-Act Structure.
* **Act I: The Roots & The Anticipation (Minutes 0:00 – 2:30)**
  * Voiceover begins (groom describing first meeting or bride's father speaking about his daughter).
  * Peaceful morning visuals: Venue setup, flower arrangements, makeup brush strokes, lehenga hanging, candid laughter during morning tea.
  * Pre-wedding rituals: Matkor earth digging, Haldi yellow bursts, mehndi henna details on hands and feet.
* **Act II: The Euphoria & Celebration (Minutes 2:30 – 6:00)**
  * High-tempo music transition (dhol beats, Bollywood dance anthem).
  * Baraat arrival, dhol players jumping, cousins dancing, groom dancing on shoulders.
  * Sangeet stage performances, crowd cheering, couple dance duet with smoke and cold pyro sparklers.
  * Grand Jaimala stage ceremony: Fireworks, flower petal shower, laughing competition to lift groom high.
* **Act III: The Sacred Vows & The Emotional Farewell (Minutes 6:00 – 10:00)**
  * Sacred Mandap: Fire crackle, Pandit chanting Sanskrit mantras in the background.
  * Kanyadaan: Father placing daughter's hand in groom's hand, close-up of folded hands and tears.
  * Saptapadi (Saat Phere) & Sindoor Daan: Pure reverence, vermilion applied along the parting, mother wiping a tear.
  * Vidai: Emotional hugs, throwing of puffed rice (*dhan*) backward, tearful car departure.
  * Epilogue: Warm smile through car window, sunset couple portrait, golden glow.

### C. Candid Video & Unposed Storytelling
* **Technique**: Telephoto lenses (85mm, 135mm, 70-200mm f/2.8) to shoot from a distance without making subjects camera-conscious.
* **Key Targets**:
  * Grandmother smiling quietly while watching grandchildren dance.
  * Groom stealing a glance at the bride during long pandit prayers.
  * Unscripted laughter when a ritual item drops or relatives tease the groom.
  * Little cousins running through the mandap with marigold garlands.
* **Pacing**: Never rush candid moments. Hold shots for 3 to 6 seconds so viewers absorb the authentic micro-expression.

### D. Static Multi-Camera & Traditional Coverage
* **Technique**: 2 to 3 fixed 4K camera angles on heavy tripods with wide and mid framing.
  * Camera A: Wide master shot of the mandap/stage.
  * Camera B: Medium close-up on the bride and groom.
  * Camera C: Roaming gimbal or medium shot on the parents and pandit.
* **Editing Rule**: Cut between angles on movement or vocal cadence. Never leave dead air or awkward pauses, but preserve the chronological integrity of ancient rituals.

### E. Master Feature Film (1 – 3 Hours)
* **Goal**: The definitive family archive.
* **Structure**:
  1. Title Sequence & Pre-Wedding Cinematic Intro (5 min)
  2. Tilak / Cheka Ceremony (15 min)
  3. Matkor & Sacred Earth Rites (15 min)
  4. Haldi Ceremony (Bride & Groom sides) (25 min)
  5. Mehndi & Sangeet Gala (30 min)
  6. Baraat Aagman & Dwar Puja (20 min)
  7. Jaimala Stage Extravaganza (25 min)
  8. Sacred Mandap (Kanyadaan, Saat Phere, Sindoor Daan) (35 min)
  9. Kohbar Chamber Rituals & Traditional Games (15 min)
  10. Vidai (Farewell) (15 min)
  11. Griha Pravesh & Post-Wedding Games (15 min)
  12. Grand Reception & Bahubhoj Stage Blessings (30 min)
  13. Credits & Blooper Reel (5 min)
