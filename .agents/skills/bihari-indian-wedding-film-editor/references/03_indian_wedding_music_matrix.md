## 2. Audio Mashup & Transition Engineering

For 5–10 minute Cinematic Trailers, using a single repetitive track causes viewer fatigue. Professional wedding trailers employ a **3-Track Mashup Strategy**:

### The 3-Track Harmonic Curve:
1. **Track 1 (The Hook & Origin)**:
   * Key: D Major / G Major.
   * Mood: Slow, romantic, acoustic, introspective (e.g., *Din Shagna Da* violin intro or *Ranjha wedding flute*).
   * Voiceover layer: Groom/Bride vow snippets placed during musical pauses.
2. **Transition Bridge (The Uplift)**:
   * Build-up: Whoosh effect + reverse cymbal + accelerating dholak roll over 4 bars.
   * Key Modulation: Shift up by a minor third or match relative minor/major.
3. **Track 2 (The Celebration & Energy Peak)**:
   * Key: E Major / A Major.
   * Mood: High-energy, joyful, dhol rhythm (e.g., *Kudmayi* upbeat chorus or *London Thumakda* drum drop).
   * Visual alignment: Fast cuts of Baraat, Sangeet dancing, and Haldi water splashes.
4. **Transition Drop (The Climax & Tears)**:
   * Sudden drop to silence or deep sub-bass boom (0.5 sec pause).
   * A single sacred bell or violin note rings out.
5. **Track 3 (The Emotional Vow & Sacred Resolution)**:
   * Mood: Grand orchestral strings with traditional Indian melody (*Dilbaro* or *O Re Piya* climax).
   * Visual alignment: Sindoor Daan, fire circumnavigation (Saat Phere), Vidai embrace, slow-motion smile into sunset.
